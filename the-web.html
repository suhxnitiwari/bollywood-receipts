Six degrees of Fabulous

Netflix's Fabulous Lives of Bollywood Wives, produced by Karan Johar's company, plugs into almost every dynasty. The original four are in pink; season 3 added Riddhima Kapoor Sahni, Shalini Passi and Kalyani Saha Chawla.

  1. Loop

    Maheep → husband Sanjay → brother Boney → son Arjun (her nephew by marriage) → ex Malaika → ex-husband Arbaaz → brother Sohail → ex-wife Seema, her co-star

  2. Circle

    Seema left fiancé Vikram Ahuja to elope with Sohail in 1998, divorced in 2022, and got back with Vikram, whose sister Tanya is Bobby Deol's wife

  3. Bhatts

    Riddhima → brother Ranbir → wife Alia → Mahesh, Pooja, Shaheen

  4. Pataudis

    Riddhima → cousin Kareena → Saif → Sharmila, Soha, Sara, Ibrahim

  5. Bachchans

    Riddhima → aunt Ritu Nanda → Nikhil → Shweta Bachchan → Amitabh, Jaya, Abhishek

  6. Next gen

    Bhavana → Chunky → Ananya and Rysa, cousins of Ahaan and Alanna
